Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production launcher icon foreground for HarmonyClash, a HarmonyOS VPN/network routing app. Create one original, exceptionally clean and recognizable abstract network-portal emblem: two bold smooth interlocking ribbon arcs form a continuous open circular tunnel with a precise diagonal passage through the center, expressing protected connection and fast flow. Strong simple silhouette, balanced negative space, rounded geometric ends. Premium flat vector-like design with only an extremely restrained mint edge accent, no glossy 3D. Colors: the main emblem is warm near-white #F4FFFC with a small mint #65C9BD accent; it will be placed on a deep teal #0E766E background. Composition: square 1024x1024 transparent canvas, emblem precisely centered and entirely contained within the central 500x500 pixel square, with generous truly transparent padding on all four sides; no colored background, no enclosing app tile, no outer shield, no tiny details. No letters, words, typography, labels, captions, watermark, border, texture, mockup or presentation board. Output only the isolated transparent emblem at full resolution, sharp smooth antialiased edges. It must be immediately readable at a 48px launcher size and survive a circular system mask.

图层导出说明
1. 使用 macOS sips 将生成原图等比缩放至 1024×1024；保留 PNG alpha，不重绘、不裁切符号。
   sips -z 1024 1024 <生成原图> --out AppScope/resources/base/media/harmonyclash_foreground.png
2. 使用本地 CoreGraphics 的 sRGB 位图上下文输出纯色 #0E766E 背景，整张保持 alpha=255；桌面圆形/圆角裁切交给鸿蒙系统。
3. 启动图在 256×256 sRGB 位图上下文中，先以边长 24% 的圆角半径裁切，再按整张画布依次绘制背景和前景；不改变符号相对位置和比例。
4. 将 AppScope 的前景、背景逐字节复制到 entry；两份对应资源的 SHA-256 相同。
5. 旧默认图标资源保留，现有引用改为新资源名。

资源验证
